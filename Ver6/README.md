# AthleteOS Ver6

A refreshed marketing website aligned to the enhanced AthleteOS product architecture.

## Finalized brand elements preserved
- AthleteOS logo concept
- Navy #102c3a + Gold #c8a45c
- The Sports Operating System for Clubs
- Play. Compete. Improve.
- Built around your club. Designed for your people.

## Ver6 information architecture
- Overview: connected product story from Play to Remember
- Platform: six-stage lifecycle with selective flip cards
- AthleteOS Live: realtime score concept, offline scoring, Arena, QR
- Competitions: tournaments, auctions, multi-sport leagues, scheduling
- Performance: ratings, rankings, H2H, PBs, records, achievements, legacy
- Inter-club: event-scoped sharing, guest club portal, microsites, sponsors
- Clubs: daily sport operations, office view, roles, cloud/private deployment
- Contact

## Interaction principles
Flip cards are used only where discovery benefits from hidden detail. Critical information remains visible. All interactions are vanilla JavaScript and the site is static-host friendly.

## Deployment
Upload the folder to any static host or GitHub Pages.
