# AthleteOS Ver5

A more interactive marketing-site concept for AthleteOS.

Finalised brand elements preserved:
- AthleteOS logo mark
- Navy + Gold colour system
- “The Sports Operating System for Clubs”
- “Play. Compete. Improve.”
- “Built around your club. Designed for your people.”

New public product story includes AthleteOS Live, arena displays, sport-aware scoring, QR venue access, tournament microsites, ratings/rankings/form, head-to-head, personal bests, club records, badges, archives, smart scheduling/conflict detection, sponsor surfaces and inter-club competition.

This is a static front-end prototype only; interactive score controls and tabs are illustrative and do not persist data.
