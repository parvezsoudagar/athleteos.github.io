The platform

Everything your club runs, in one place

Web for administrators and members. A mobile app for members and match-day scorers.

Bring your whole membership across in one afternoon

Import from Excel or CSV with a preview, row-level errors and duplicate detection before anything is saved.

  • Your own membership number format, such as A-107-P or AA-888-X
  • Families linked by membership number and kept through import
  • Bulk member logins with a must-change first password
  • One login for members of several clubs; choose the club at sign-in
  • Self-service password reset by email

Fill your courts, tables and lanes

Conflict-free booking in your club's timezone, following your own rules for slot length, advance window and cancellation.

  • Venues and up to 50 courts, tables or lanes at a time
  • Weekly opening hours and maintenance closures
  • Club, sport and resource booking rules
  • Waitlist offers when a slot frees up
  • Booking export and personal calendar download

Run coaching programmes without the admin

Recurring weekly programmes generate sessions automatically; members register and cancel themselves.

  • Coach profiles per sport
  • Capacity limits with ordered waitlists
  • Coaches review rankings and verified history

Live scoring

Score on any phone, even with no signal

Sport-specific screens on mobile, plus a web scoring desk. Admins assign scorers to each match from the web.

Offline-first

Every action is stored on the phone instantly and survives app restarts. Sync sends it in order when signal returns.

Never counted twice

A dropped connection mid-sync is safe: the server recognises events it already has.

Conflict-safe

Two phones on one match? The server keeps one official sequence and the other phone is told, never silently merged.

Server is the referee

The official score is always calculated on the server; the phone shows a clearly labelled preview.

Sport-specific screens

Rally points, tennis tie-breaks, snooker breaks, cricket ball-by-ball, darts, chess, golf holes and finish times.

Audited corrections

Undo needs a reason; finalised results change only through a recorded correction chain.

Questions

Good to know

Do we need our own server?

No. Each club gets its own branded address and a private database on the AthleteOS platform. We provision it for you.

What happens if the venue has no Wi-Fi?

Scorers keep scoring on the phone. Every action is saved on the device and synced in order when the connection returns.

Can members belong to more than one club?

Yes. A member uses one email and password and chooses the club when signing in.

Can we use our own club name on emails?

Yes. Club emails go out with your club's sender name and your reply-to address.

Is it ready for official events?

AthleteOS is in a supervised pilot. We validate your sports, rules and first tournament with you before official use.
