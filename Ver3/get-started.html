A few good questions

Before we begin.

Can we use our own club name and logo?+

Yes. Club identity is configured during onboarding. The product also supports a primary event sponsor on tournament views.

Can one membership include a family?+

Yes. Yes. Family relationships stay connected while each person keeps their own sporting profile. Junior participation includes guardian consent controls.

Does AthleteOS collect booking or coaching payments?+

No. Fee information supports an external club billing workflow, staff confirmation and scheduled summaries. Auctions use virtual team-budget credits.

Can we run it on our own server?+

The product supports club-managed or cloud deployment with a dedicated club database. Server sizing, backups, email, TLS and scheduler configuration must be agreed and tested for the club.

Is the mobile app ready for public app stores?+

The native mobile experience is in development. We will demonstrate the supported web and mobile workflows available for your club’s pilot.

Are all official rules for all sports implemented?+

Coverage varies by sport and format. We will confirm the supported rules in your demo; advanced specialist scoring remains on the roadmap.

What is planned next?+

Richer sport-specific scoring, advanced handicaps, a more complete mobile experience and live video score overlays, including planned YouTube integration.

How is my enquiry sent?+

The form prepares an email in your own email app. You review and send it yourself. This website does not store your form entries or send them to a server. You can also write directly to contact@AthleteOS.club.

Website privacy

This website uses no tracking cookies or third-party analytics. Enquiry details stay in your browser until you choose to open and send an email through your email provider. Hosting providers may maintain access logs. For privacy questions, contact contact@AthleteOS.club.
