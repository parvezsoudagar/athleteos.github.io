The platform

Club life has many moving parts.
Give them one place.

From the membership desk to the court, thoughtful tools help everyone know what comes next. Find more time for the part that matters: playing.

Member & family records

02 / People

Safe, accurate profiles

Keep member details accurate, give staff the right controls and support younger players with guardian consent.

Bring your existing membership into AthleteOS with a guided, reviewed onboarding journey.

04 / People

Progress worth recognising

Turn results into a personal sporting story, with performance history and ratings that reflect recent play.

05 / People

A badge for every journey

06 / Play

Court time, made simple

Help members find their next session, with bookable courts and venue policies that keep access fair.

07 / Play

Coaching that keeps going

Bring regular training into the member experience with monthly plans, recurring renewals and helpful reminders.

08 / Play

Billing without a payment gateway

Give club staff clear booking and coaching summaries for existing billing processes. AthleteOS does not collect payments.

09 / Play

Keep everyone in the loop

Keep captains ready and members informed with upcoming-game reminders, announcements and configured email or push notifications.

10 / Competition

Formats for different ambitions

From a friendly ladder to a club championship, choose a format that fits and reuse a previous event as a fresh draft.

11 / Competition

League roles with purpose

Give organisers, owners, captains and scorers a clear place in the season, from team building to match-day coordination.

12 / Competition

Rules that fit your club

Adapt eligibility, scoring and handicaps to supported club formats, with broader specialist sport coverage in development.

13 / Operations

Useful reports, ready to share

Share useful participant lists, player cards, results and histories through Excel and PDF exports.

14 / Operations

Your brand, on your screens

Keep your club identity front and centre, with your logo and event sponsor branding across supported views.

15 / Operations

An independent home for your data

Keep private club records separate, with controlled access and hosting options suited to your club.

16 / Operations

Setup and continuity

Start with guided setup and keep operations moving with controlled administrator recovery and handover.

17 / Operations

Improvements with oversight

Let your club’s recurring needs inform a reviewed improvement roadmap, with people responsible for every change.

Beyond your home club

Bring invited clubs together through a shared competition experience, while keeping their domestic records separate.
